# Sleepy Sheep privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Sleepy Sheep is a focus timer extension. It does not collect, send, or sell any personal information.

**What it stores.** Your timer settings, your grass count, your session count for the day, and where you placed the sheep on screen. All of this is saved on your own device using the browser's extension storage. It is never uploaded anywhere.

**What it does not do.** It has no account, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. It makes no network requests. It does not read, record or send the content of the pages you visit, your browsing history, your keystrokes or anything you type.

**Website access.** If you allow it, the extension adds the sheep widget to the pages you choose. It only draws the widget on the page. It does not read the page. You can allow all websites or only some, and you can remove access at any time from the extension's popup or the browser's extension settings.

**Notifications.** If you leave notifications on, it shows a browser notification when a focus session or break ends. You can turn this off in the popup.
