Sleepy Sheep is ready

One last step: choose where your sheep can show up. It will sit in the corner of the page, study while your timer runs, and sleep when you take a break.

  • Chrome will ask you to confirm. The sheep only draws itself on the page.
  • It does not read what is on your pages, and nothing leaves your browser.
  • You can change this any time from the sheep icon in your toolbar.
